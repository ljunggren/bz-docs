### Major Release 9

In this release, we introduce source control in the style of GIT. This includes a new source control view, with support for commit, stash, and revert. We simplify the side-bar and add an options tab for action details. In action details, we also consolidate pre-script, loop script, and result script in a single "script" tab.

![](https://boozang.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/release-8.2-1.jpg)

[](https://youtu.be/Hp_IT1IC2ZI)
